🏋️ Training Plan

Combination of strength training, kettlebell, Judo Randori and Surf Finisher

Monday

Strength A – Back & Grip + Surf Finisher

Tuesday

Judo Randori (1.5h)

Wednesday

Kettlebell Legs

Thursday

Strength C – Chest & Shoulders + Surf Finisher

Friday

Judo Randori (1.5h)

Saturday

Rest / Mobility

Sunday

Kettlebell Shoulders + Core

💪 Strength A – Back & Grip

  • Scap Pull-ups – 2×10
  • Band Pull-Aparts – 2×15
  • Pull-Ups – 5×5–8
  • Horizontal Pull-Ups – 4×6–8
  • Dumbbell Rows – 3×10 / side
  • One-Arm Rows – 3×10 / side
  • Reverse Flys / Face Pulls – 3×15
  • Hanging Knee Raises / Toes-to-Bar – 3×max
  • + Surf Finisher

🏋️ Strength C – Chest & Shoulders

  • Dips – 5×6–10
  • One-Arm Shoulder Press – 4×6–8
  • Slow Push-Ups – 3×12–15
  • Lateral Raises – 3×15
  • Windmills – 3×6–8 / side
  • Plank Shoulder Taps – 3×40 sec
  • + Surf Finisher

🦵 Kettlebell – Legs

  • Goblet Squat – 12
  • Kettlebell Swings – 20
  • Reverse Lunges – 8 / leg
  • Kettlebell Romanian Deadlift – 12
  • Squat Jumps or Box Jumps – 6
  • 4 rounds – 60–90 sec rest

💥 Kettlebell – Shoulders & Core

  • Kettlebell Push Press – 6 / arm
  • Kettlebell Halo – 10 per direction
  • Kettlebell Slingshot Stop – 20 alternations
  • Kettlebell Windmill – 6 / side
  • Suitcase Carry – 15 / side
  • Kettlebell Russian Twists – 20
  • 4 rounds – 60–90 sec rest

🏄 Surf Finisher Description

  • Pop-Ups – 6–8 reps
  • Superman Swimmers – 20–30 sec
  • Mountain Climbers – 40 reps
  • Plank to Push-Up – 10 reps
  • Burpees – 6–8 reps
  • Recommended rest: 30–40 sec between rounds
  • Rounds: 4–5 rounds, 2× per week after Back or Shoulder day

📈 Progression

  • +2 reps per exercise per week
  • or add one extra round
  • Every 5–6 weeks take a lighter week
